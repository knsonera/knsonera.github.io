1. What Don't You Dare is (and isn't)

Don't You Dare suggests one small dare a day to help you practice courage, plus optional feel-good "Recharge" tasks. The dares are suggestions. You decide whether, when and how to do them. The app is not therapy, and it isn't medical, mental-health or fitness advice. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services.

2. Dare safely

  • Only do a dare if it's safe for you and for the people around you. Skip or swap any dare that isn't: "Can't today" is there for exactly that.
  • Respect other people. If a dare involves someone else, be kind, take no for an answer, and don't film or post anyone without their permission.
  • Follow the law and the rules of the places you're in.
  • If you have a health condition, check with a professional before physical or food dares.

You're responsible for what you choose to do. To the extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for injury, loss or damage that results from doing a dare.
