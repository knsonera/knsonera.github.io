Math Game for Kids 5–10

Math Haunt

A spooky-friendly mental math game. Friendly ghosts float in carrying sums: type the answer to catch them, and light up a haunted mansion one room at a time.

Play Now

Why Kids Keep Playing

Math Haunt turns fact practice into a game of catch. Practice mode has no clock, so younger children can take their time. Blitz mode is a quick 75-second round for kids who want a challenge. The difficulty adapts as they play, so it stays just hard enough.

What They Learn

  • The Garden (ages 5–7): adding and taking away up to 20, and making 10.
  • Ground Floor (ages 7–9): doubles and halves, adding tens, two-digit sums, making 100 and adding three numbers.
  • First Floor (ages 7–10): times tables from 2 to 10, missing numbers and multiplying by 10.
  • Attic & Tower (ages 8–10): dividing, square numbers and a mixed challenge room.
  • A wrong answer brings a picture hint (dots, ten-frames, arrays) and a strategy, not a penalty.
  • Badges, stars and a high-score board on the device, plus an optional read-aloud voice for early readers.

Privacy and Safety

The game has no analytics, no cookies, no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-in. Nothing your child types leaves the device: progress, badges and the high-score board are saved only in the browser's local storage, and clearing the browser's site data removes them.

This information page on mindgameslab.com uses Google Analytics to count visits. The game itself does not.

Best For

Children aged 5 to 10 practicing math facts at home, on a tablet or a computer with a keyboard. It also works on a phone held sideways.
