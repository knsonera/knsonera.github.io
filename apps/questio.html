QuestiO: Habit Quests

QuestiO is the habit app where you decide how your friends show up for you. Not another group chat, not another streak to break — just a few people who notice, supporting you the way you actually asked to be supported.

Join the iOS beta Join Android Waitlist

Why It Works

Showing up is easier when someone kind notices. But most apps decide for you how that support should feel, and get it wrong — a cheerful nag on the day you needed space, or silence on the day you needed a push. QuestiO lets you say it outright. On any quest you tell the friends in your corner how you want to be treated when you slip, and they are shown what you asked for first. Support stops being a guess. And a missed day never erases anything: QuestiO counts what you did, so weeks build instead of breaking.

What You Can Do

  • Set a support pact on any quest: a gentle reminder, a push, the tiny version, a check-in, or space. Change it whenever you like.
  • Create quests for any habit, from fitness and reading to mindfulness and self-care, on your own days of the week.
  • Check in with one tap the moment you show up. It works with no signal at all, and a two-minute day still counts as the tiny version.
  • Ask a friend to have your back without joining your quest, or pick a buddy and do it together.
  • Miss a couple of days and get a kind “let’s restart” that asks what got in the way before it does anything at all.

Best For

People who want accountability without pressure. Who would rather have one friend quietly notice their effort than a feed that scores it. Who have bounced off apps that punish a missed day.
