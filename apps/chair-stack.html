Stacking Game

Chair Stack

A chair swings above the tower. Drop it at the right moment, land it on the one below, and see how high you can stack before it all comes down.

Play Now

How to Play

  • Press Space, click or tap to drop each chair onto the one below.
  • Off-center chairs make the tower lean, and real physics decides whether it holds.
  • Every chair that falls uses up one of your three misses.
  • The swing gets faster and wider as the tower grows.
  • Make the global Top 10 and save your score with three letters, arcade style.

Made by a Young Developer

Chair Stack was designed and built by a young game developer, and Mind Games Lab is proud to host it.

Privacy and Safety

The game has no analytics, no cookies, no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-in. Your own best score is saved only in the browser's local storage on your device.

The global Top 10 stores only what a player chooses to save: three letters and a score, nothing else. No names, no accounts, no device details. Scores are kept in Google Firebase and can't be edited by anyone once saved.

This information page on mindgameslab.com uses Google Analytics to count visits. The game itself does not.
