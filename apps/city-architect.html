Maths & Physics Game for Kids 7+

City Architect

Learn maths and physics by building a city. Help the mayor turn empty land into streets, a park, a bridge and a school, one building puzzle at a time.

Play Now

How It Works

Every part of the city is a short puzzle. Mix the right concrete for a foundation, count the bricks for the walls, balance a crane, load a truck, fence a park. Earn coins to unlock new areas, and come back for new numbers each round. A hint button helps when a puzzle gets stuck, and the difficulty shifts with how each topic is going.

What They Learn

  • Construction site: ratios and area (foundation, walls, roof), pipe logic and electrical power.
  • Sunny Park: perimeter and the fence-post problem.
  • River Bridge: moments on a crane and exact sums with physics crates.
  • Hilltop School: surface area and paint coverage, and volume for a water tank.
  • Harder levels bring in Pythagoras, decimals and I = P / V.

Privacy and Safety

The game has no analytics, no cookies, no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-in. Progress is saved only in the browser's local storage on your device, and clearing the browser's site data removes it.

This information page on mindgameslab.com uses Google Analytics to count visits. The game itself does not.

Best For

Children aged 7 and up, on a tablet or a computer. Everything works by tap or drag; on a phone, hold it sideways.
