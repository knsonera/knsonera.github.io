One brave thing a day

Don't You Dare

Every day everyone gets the same small, real dare. Rate how scary it feels, do it, then rate how scary it actually was. Watch the gap: that's courage, measured. Free, no account, no ads.

Download on iOS Get on Android

Why People Love It

Big goals are easy to put off. One small dare isn't. Don’t You Dare turns courage into a daily habit that's fun to keep and fun to brag about, and it's forgiving on the days life gets in the way.

What You Can Do

  • Do the dare of the day: 366 dares across social, food, style, nature, learning, switch-ups and more.
  • Rate your fear before and after, and see it shrink over time in your Win log.
  • Can't today? Say why (at work, no money, alone, stuck indoors) and pick from two dares that fit. No streak loss.
  • Hold to dare, get the stamp, and share a brag card with friends.
  • Level up from Warm-up to Fearless, or take on the 14-day Fear Sprint.
  • Running on empty? The 14-day Recharge sprint swaps dares for small feel-good things for body and mind.
  • Keep your streak with two freezes a week, plus a bonus when you come back.

Best For

Anyone who wants to feel braver, try new things and get out of a rut, in tiny daily steps they can actually do. Works on iPhone, iPad and Android.
