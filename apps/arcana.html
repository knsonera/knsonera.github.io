Arcana

Tarot as a mirror, not a prediction. Ask about something real and draw a card: you get one pattern you may be missing, one question worth sitting with and one small move. Commit to it, and tomorrow Arcana asks whether you did it. Free, no account, nothing to cancel.

Why It Matters

Most reflection apps give you an insight and stop there. Arcana asks the next day. The card is a lens, not a forecast: it names a pattern you may be living right now, then hands you one thing to do about it. "No" is a useful answer too. Arcana asks what got in the way, and over time shows you what keeps getting in the way.

What You Can Do

  • Ask about work, money, a relationship, a decision, yourself or creative work, and get a reading written for that part of your life.
  • Tap "Push harder" when you want the less comfortable version of the same reading.
  • Commit to one move by tonight, tomorrow or this week, and answer "Did you?" the next day in one tap. Add the check-in to your calendar, or send a card to one friend who will ask you.
  • Log a card from your own deck, upright or reversed, and get the same pattern, question and move.
  • Work through a bigger choice in a Decision Session, or spend 22 days with the Major Arcana, one card a day.
  • Save a backup, a readable copy or a printable month of your readings.

How It's Made

No AI in the app. Every reading is fixed text, edited and approved by a real human at Mind Games Lab. Same card, same words, every time. No streaks, no ads, no trials. Your questions, reflections and commitments never leave your device. The rules Arcana keeps are written down in its promises.

Best For

People who already know what they should do and keep not doing it. Tarot readers who want a place to log their own pulls. Anyone who wants a daily mirror without predictions or a subscription. Works in any modern browser, on a phone or a computer.
